# Getting Started with Orbit

Orbit helps you stay close to the people who matter. You tell it how often you'd like to be in touch with someone, you jot down when you catch up, and each day it suggests a few people to reach out to — with a reason, not a guilt trip.

This guide walks through everything from your first five minutes to the power-user shortcuts. You don't need to read it all: sections 1–4 cover what most people use every day.

## 1. The idea in one minute

Orbit has three ideas:

| Idea | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Closeness** | For each person you care about, choose how often you'd like to be in touch — weekly, monthly, every few months, yearly, or never. |
| **Logs** | When you catch up — a call, a coffee, a message — write a quick note about it. One line is enough. |
| **Today** | Orbit compares the two and suggests a few people each day who could use a hello, and says why. |

Everything else — facts, groups, reminders — makes those three work better.

**Where things live**

- **iPhone:** tabs along the bottom — **Today · Log · People · Groups · Settings**.
- **iPad and Mac:** a sidebar with **Today, Log, People, Groups**. Settings is in the sidebar on iPad and under **Orbit → Settings…** (⌘,) on Mac. Press **⌘K** anywhere to log.

## 2. Your first five minutes

The first time you open Orbit, a short welcome explains the basics and offers three ways in:

- **Add someone** — start with the people you think of first. Five to ten is plenty.
- **Import from Contacts** — brings in names, phone numbers, emails, companies and birthdays from your address book (Settings → Import). Imported people stay quiet until you choose their closeness, so you won't wake up to 200 "overdue" contacts.
- **Try sample data** — eight made-up people with history, so you can explore. You can clear it later in Settings.

You can also turn on **weekly reminder and birthdays** here (see [Reminders](#14-reminders-and-birthdays)).

**A good first session**

1. Add or import a handful of people.
2. If you imported, tap **Set closeness** on Today and choose a closeness for the people who matter. Tap **Leave the rest quiet** for everyone else.
3. Go to the **Log** tab and type one thing that happened recently, like *Called Mum yesterday about her trip*.
4. Come back to **Today** tomorrow.

## 3. Logging in plain words

This is the heart of Orbit. On iPhone, open the **Log** tab — the box at the bottom is ready to type. On iPad and Mac, press **⌘K** (or the ✎ button) from anywhere.

Just type what happened, the way you'd say it:

```text
Coffee with Sarah yesterday, talked about her move
Called Mum about her surgery
Dinner with Sam, Alex and Jo on Friday
Texted my siblings about Mama going overseas
Drinks with the book club last night #fun
```

As you type, Orbit shows a **preview** of what it understood. Nothing is saved until you press **Return** or the **↑** send button.

### The preview

| Row | What it shows | How to change it |
|---|---|---|
| **With** | Who you were with, as chips. | Tap ✕ on a chip to remove it. Tap **Add** to add someone. See the chip types below. |
| **What** | The kind of contact — Message, Call, Coffee, Meal, Meeting, Video Call, Visit, or your own words. | Tap any suggestion chip. If nothing was found, Orbit asks you to pick one. |
| **When** | When it happened. Blank means *now*. | Tap the date to change the day and time. |
| **Note** | Everything else you wrote. | Edit your text above. |
| **Tags** | Any `#words` you typed. | Edit your text above. |
| **Also** | Facts spotted in your note, like *he's moving to Berlin*. | Tick the ones you want saved. They're never saved unless you tick them. |

**Person chips**

- **Sarah Connor** — found. Tap ✕ to remove.
- **Which Sam?** (orange) — more than one person matches. Tap and choose.
- **Who's your sister?** (orange) — you wrote a relationship word Orbit doesn't know yet. Tap, choose the person, and Orbit remembers.
- **New: Jamie Lee** — no one by that name. Saving will add them as a new person. Tap for options: *It's someone I've added…* (pick the right person and Orbit remembers the name) or *Not a person here*.
- **Not a person here** — use this when Orbit mistook a word for a person or group. The word goes back into your note.

If you type just a name — *Sarah* — the preview offers **Open Sarah** to jump to her page.

### Writing so Orbit understands

Orbit reads the **start** of your sentence for who, what and when, and treats the rest as the note.

- **A comma, full stop, dash or semicolon** starts the note: *Coffee with Sarah, she got the job*.
- **Topic words** also start the note: *about, regarding, re, that, because, to ask, to tell, to discuss*. So in *Called Tom about Mama's birthday*, Tom is the person and *about Mama's birthday* is the note.
- **Lists of people** work with commas and "and": *Lunch with Sam, Alex and Jo*.
- **Names** can be full (*Sarah Connor*), first names (*Sarah*), or nicknames you've taught it (*Mama*).
- **Relationship words** work once Orbit knows them: *my sister, my parents, my siblings, my boss* (see [Relationships](#9-relationships-and-nicknames)).
- **Groups** are found by their name or a word from it after *my / the / with*: *texted my siblings* finds a group called *Three Siblings*.
- **Tags**: add `#words` anywhere.

**Dates and times Orbit understands**

| You write | Orbit reads |
|---|---|
| today, just now, this morning, this evening, tonight | today |
| yesterday, yesterday evening, last night | yesterday |
| 3 days ago, a week ago, two weeks ago, a couple of days ago | that many days/weeks/months back |
| last week, last month, last weekend | roughly then |
| Monday, on Friday, last Saturday | the most recent one |
| 3 March, March 3rd, 2026-03-03, 3/3 | that date (the most recent one if no year) |
| …at 3pm, at 9:30am, at 15:00 | that time |

Numeric dates like 3/4 follow your device's region (3 April in the UK, March 4 in the US). Orbit never logs something in the future.

### Logging with full details

For a longer entry, use the **Log** form instead of typing: tap **Log** on Today or a person's page. It has the same fields — people, what, note, tags, and date *and* time — with suggestion chips for actions and tags you've used before.

## 4. Today

Today answers one question: *who could use a hello?*

**Today's list.** Up to five people, chosen once each morning so the list doesn't reshuffle while you work through it. Each row has:

- a **reason** in plain words — *"You used to talk every 2 weeks — it's been 6"* or *"It's been 5 weeks. You'd like to be in touch monthly."*
- a **Log** button,
- a **⋯** menu with **Quick log** (one tap, no form), **Snooze** (a week, a month, three months), **Closeness**, and **Open**.

On iPhone you can also **swipe right to log** and **swipe left to snooze** for a week.

As you log or snooze, rows tick off. When everyone's done, Today says you're caught up.

**More.** Others who could use a hello, folded away so the list stays short. Tap to show them.

**Coming up.** Birthdays and anniversaries in the next two weeks, from people's facts.

**Latest.** Your five most recent logs. Swipe to edit.

**Set closeness card.** If you imported people and haven't chosen their closeness yet, a card at the top lets you do it in one sheet.

### How Orbit decides who to suggest

- Only people whose closeness is set, or whom you've logged with, are considered. Archived, snoozed and Deep Space people never appear.
- Someone is suggested when they're **past their closeness** (for example, more than a month for Near Orbit), or when you've been in touch **less often than you used to**, even if you're not overdue yet.
- Closer people rank higher for the same gap.

## 5. Closeness

Closeness sets how often you'd like to be in touch. Names are shown bolder the closer someone is.

| Closeness | Rhythm | Typical people |
|---|---|---|
| **Inner Circle** | Weekly | Partner, family, best friends |
| **Near Orbit** | Monthly | Close friends you see regularly |
| **Mid Orbit** | Every few months | Good friends, active colleagues |
| **Outer Orbit** | Yearly | Acquaintances, old friends |
| **Deep Space** | No reminders | People you just want on record |

Change it from:

- the **closeness chip** under a person's name on their page,
- **⋯ → Closeness** on Today,
- right-click / long-press in **People**,
- the person's **Edit** form,
- **Select** several people in People and choose **Edit…** to change them all at once.

People added by hand have their closeness set. People imported from Contacts don't until you choose — that's what keeps Today calm on day one.

## 6. People

Everyone you've added.

- **Search** looks at names, notes, facts, tags, nicknames and group names — try *google*, *jazz* or *book club*.
- **Filters** (the filter button at the top) narrow the list: *Due for a catch-up*, closeness, group, tag, has logs / no logs, has facts / no facts, and *Include archived*.
- **Sort** (iPhone): name, last contact, closeness, or most overdue. On Mac, click a column header.
- **Swipe right to log**, **swipe left to archive** (iPhone). Right-click on Mac for the same and more.
- **Select** (iPhone) or ⌘-click (Mac) several people, then **Edit…** to change closeness, archive or restore, add to or remove from groups, or delete — all at once.
- **+** adds a new person.

**Archive vs delete.** Archiving hides someone everywhere but keeps their history and can be undone. Deleting removes them, their facts and their history for good.

## 7. A person's page

- **Name and status.** A plain-language line — *"It's been 6 weeks…"* or *"Last in touch last week — on track."*
- **Closeness chip** and, if snoozed, a **Snoozed until…** chip (tap to unsnooze).
- **Your sister · aka Mama** — their relationship and nicknames, if you've set any.
- **Three action tiles:**
  - **Log** opens the full log form.
  - **Quick log** saves a Call, Message, Coffee… in one tap.
  - **Add fact** opens the fact form.
- **Notes** — anything you wrote about them on the Edit form.
- **Facts** — grouped into Personal, Professional, Other and Contact Info. Tap to edit; swipe to delete. Birthdays coming up soon show *In 3 days*.
- **Activity** — their history by month. Tap an entry to edit; swipe to delete. The 🔍 button in the toolbar searches and filters the history by action.

The toolbar also has **Edit** (name, closeness, notes, nicknames) and **⋯** for snooze, archive and delete.

## 8. Facts

Facts are the details worth remembering: birthdays, kids' names, where someone works, what they're allergic to.

### Adding facts in plain words

Type them in the Log tab like anything else:

```text
Sarah's birthday is 3 March
Alex's kids are Ava and Leo
Tom works at Google
Tom is a designer
Priya lives in Austin          (or: Priya moved to Austin)
Sam likes jazz and sushi
Sam hates olives
remember that Sarah is allergic to peanuts
Jo has a dog called Rex
Ben is my brother
my sister's birthday is 14 July
```

The preview shows each fact with a checkbox. Untick any you don't want, then save.

**Facts inside logs.** If a log mentions something lasting — *Coffee with Tom, he's moving to Berlin and got a job at Spotify* — the preview offers **Also save city: Berlin** and **company: Spotify**. They're only saved if you tick them.

### Adding facts by hand

Tap **Add fact** on a person's page. A fact can be a single value (*city: Austin*) or a list (*likes: jazz, sushi*). You can also choose a category, or let Orbit pick one from the name.

### Birthdays and anniversaries

Any fact whose name includes *birthday* or *anniversary* is read as a date. Formats like *3 March*, *March 3*, *3/15* and *1990-03-15* all work. These appear under **Coming up** on Today and, if reminders are on, as a notification on the day.

## 9. Relationships and nicknames

### Relationships

A person's relationship to you is a fact called **relationship** — for example *sister*, *mum*, *older brother* or *boss*. Once it's set, you can log with relationship words:

| You write | Orbit finds |
|---|---|
| my sister, my sis | whoever's relationship is sister |
| my mum / mom / mama | whoever's relationship is mother |
| my parents | your mother and father |
| my siblings | everyone whose relationship is sister or brother (or a group of that name — see below) |
| my kids | your sons and daughters |
| my boss, my colleague, my neighbour, my best friend | the matching person |

Set a relationship by:

- typing *Ben is my brother*,
- adding a **relationship** fact by hand, or
- just using the word. The first time you write *my sister*, the preview asks **Who's your sister?** — pick her once and Orbit saves the relationship.

### Nicknames ("Also known as")

People and groups can have other names: *Mama*, *Grandma Lim*, *the sibs*. Orbit uses them when reading what you type.

- **Orbit learns them for you.** When you tell the preview who a name means — choosing *Margaret* for *Mama*, or a group for *siblings* — it remembers.
- **Edit them** on the person's or group's Edit form, under **Also known as** (comma-separated).
- A nickname can belong to **several people** at once — give Ava and Leo the nickname *the twins*, and *played with the twins* logs with both.

## 10. Groups

Groups are people you often see together: a team, a family, a book club.

- **Create a group** with **+** on the Groups tab. Give it a name, optional notes, nicknames and members.
- **Add someone to a group** from the group page (**Add**), on the group's Edit form, in bulk from People with **Select → Edit…**, or by typing `@Sarah *Book Club`.
- **Log for everyone** at once from the group page, or just type it: *Dinner with the book club*, *Texted my siblings*. Each member gets the log on their own page.
- **The group page** shows members (the ones due for a catch-up first), the group's shared history, and its nicknames. **⋯ → Edit group** changes the name, nicknames and members. **⋯ → Select Members** removes several at once.
- **Deleting a group** keeps its people and their history.

**How groups are recognised when you type**

- the group's full name — *Book Club*,
- one of its nicknames — *the readers*,
- a word from its name after *my / our / the / with / and* — *my siblings* finds *Three Siblings*. A word on its own at the start of a sentence (*Book with Tom*) won't trigger a group.

If more than one group matches, the preview asks which. Pick one and Orbit remembers.

## 11. The Log tab and your history

The Log tab reads like a conversation: everything you've logged, newest at the bottom, grouped by day, with the composer right above the keyboard.

- **Tap an entry** to edit it — people, action, note, tags, date and time. For an entry logged with several people, you can change it for everyone at once.
- **Long-press** to open a person or delete the entry.
- **All activity** (the list button at the top) opens the full history with search and multi-select, to add or remove tags or delete many entries at once.

Messages and confirmations for things that don't appear in the history — like saving a fact — show just above the composer.

## 12. Re-read with Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 / macOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings), a **✨ Re-read with Apple Intelligence** button appears under the preview.

- Use it when Orbit's first reading isn't right — an unusual phrasing, a long run-on sentence.
- It runs Apple's **on-device** model. Your text doesn't leave your device.
- The new reading is marked **Read by Apple Intelligence**. Names are still checked against your real contacts and groups, and nothing is saved until you confirm. **Use original** switches back.
- If Apple Intelligence is off, still downloading or not supported, the button area tells you why. Orbit's normal reading always works without it.

## 13. Undo

Every change — logs, edits, facts, closeness, snooze, archive, group membership — shows a banner with **Undo** for a few seconds.

You can also:

- type **!undo** in the Log tab, or
- press **⌥⌘Z** on Mac.

Undo goes back one step at a time. Permanent deletes (a person, a group, or erasing data) can't be undone and always ask first.

## 14. Reminders and birthdays

Turn on **Weekly reminder and birthdays** in Settings → Reminders (or during the welcome).

- **Sunday evening (6pm)** — one notification naming a few people who could use a hello.
- **Birthdays and anniversaries** — a notification at 9am on the day, for date facts in the next two months.

Reminders are scheduled on your device and refreshed each time you open Orbit. If nothing arrives, check that notifications are allowed for Orbit in your device's Settings.

## 15. Settings, import and backup

- **How Orbit works** — a one-page summary of this guide.
- **Sample data** — load or clear the demo people.
- **Reminders** — see above.
- **Tags** — rename tags everywhere at once.
- **Import from Contacts** — choose who to bring in from your address book. Duplicates are skipped.
- **Export** — a full **JSON backup** (people, facts, history, nicknames, closeness) or a **CSV** summary for spreadsheets.
- **Import a JSON backup** — merge into your current data or replace everything.
- **Appearance** — light, dark or system, and an accent colour.
- **Maintenance** — check the database, rebuild Spotlight search, and permanently purge deleted logs.
- **Erase all data** — removes everything. This can't be undone.

> Note: JSON backups save each person's group names but not groups as separate records, so group nicknames aren't included.

## 16. Shortcut syntax (optional)

Plain words are the easiest way to use Orbit. If you prefer exact commands, the Log tab also understands symbols at the **start of a word**:

| Symbol | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `@` | person | `@Sarah`, `@"Sarah Connor"` |
| `!` | action | `!Coffee`, `!Call` |
| `#` | tag | `#work` |
| `"…"` | note (unquoted leftover words count too) | `"talked about the move"` |
| `^` | when | `^yesterday`, `^3d`, `^2w`, `^last-monday`, `^"last monday"` |
| `*` | group | `*Family` (add to), `*-Family` (remove from) |
| `>` | fact | `> birthday: 3 March` |

**Examples**

```text
@Sarah !Coffee #catch-up "fun chat" ^yesterday   log
@Sarah coffee yesterday                          mix: person by @, the rest in plain words
@Tom > birthday: 3 March                         set a fact
@Tom > likes + Jazz                              add to a list fact
@Tom > likes - Jazz                              remove from a list fact
@Tom > likes: void                               delete a fact
@Sarah *Family                                   add to a group
*Family !Dinner #holiday                         log for the whole group
@Sam !archive    /    @Sam !restore              archive or restore
!undo                                            undo the last change
```

Because symbols only count at the start of a word, values like `tom@gmail.com` or `Rock & Roll!` are kept as typed. An unrecognised time like `^banana` shows an error rather than guessing.

## 18. Privacy

- No account, no sign-up and no ads.
- Your data is stored on your device and synced through **your own iCloud** account.
- Re-read uses Apple's **on-device** model; nothing is sent to Orbit or anyone else.
- Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
- People can appear in Spotlight search on your device. Archived people are removed from it, and you can rebuild the index in Settings → Maintenance.

## 19. Tips and troubleshooting

**Make it a habit**

- Log right after you hang up or leave — one line is enough.
- Glance at Today once a day. Snooze freely; it's there to help, not to nag.
- Keep closeness honest. If someone's always overdue and that's fine, move them out one level.
- Add a fact the moment you hear it: *Sam's daughter starts school in September*.

**"Orbit picked the wrong person"**

Tap the chip and choose the right one, or **Not a person here**. Orbit remembers your choice for next time. To start a note earlier, use a comma or a word like *about*.

**"It made a new person I didn't mean"**

Before saving, tap the **New: …** chip and choose **It's someone I've added…** or **Not a person here**. After saving, use **Undo**.

**"Today is empty"**

Set closeness for the people who matter (imported people start unset), or log a few catch-ups so Orbit learns your rhythm.

**"Today shows people I don't need to hear about"**

Move them to **Deep Space** (no reminders), snooze them, or archive them.

**"My sister / my siblings isn't recognised"**

Add a **relationship** fact (*sister*, *brother*…) to the right people, type *Ben is my brother*, or pick them when the preview asks *Who's your sister?*.

**"No birthday notifications"**

Check that the fact's name contains *birthday* or *anniversary*, that the date is in a format like *3 March*, that reminders are on in Settings, and that notifications are allowed for Orbit.

**"I don't see Re-read"**

It needs iOS 26 / macOS 26 or later on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. It's also hidden when you're using shortcut syntax.
